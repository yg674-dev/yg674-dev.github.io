About Me

I'm an AI and platform product manager with 4+ years across TikTok, ServiceNow, and Tencent Cloud, building agentic products, governance platforms, and enterprise ecosystems. I work at the layer where a probabilistic system meets an organization that needs it to be accountable — retrieval evals, failure diagnostics, human-in-the-loop guardrails, and the distribution primitives that make third-party agents auditable at enterprise scale.

Before business school I spent two years at Tencent Cloud building the TenDi Intelligent Decision Platform, a fintech AI risk-control product running 200,000 risk assessments a day for financial institutions across 10+ countries. I then moved to the investment side as an Investment Project Manager, leading due diligence and Series A/B rounds for deep-tech startups — which taught me to read a product through its market and its economics, not just its roadmap.

I hold a Master's in Applied Data Science from USC and a BS in Math & Economics from UC San Diego, and I thrive in environments where data, strategy, and execution converge.
