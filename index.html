About Me

I'm an AI product manager working at the layer where fragmented organizational knowledge becomes something an AI system can reliably use. Most recently at TikTok, I designed agentic knowledge infrastructure for LIVE ecosystem governance — consolidating thousands of scattered risk features into a queryable foundation, and pairing every AI recommendation with evidence, confidence signals, and human approval before anything reaches production.

Before business school I spent two years at Tencent Cloud building the TenDi Intelligent Decision Platform, a fintech AI risk-control product running 200,000 risk assessments a day for financial institutions across 20+ countries. I then moved to the investment side as an Investment Manager, leading due diligence and Series A/B rounds for deep-tech startups — which taught me to read a product through its market and its economics, not just its roadmap.

I hold a Master's in Applied Data Science from USC and a BS in Math & Economics from UC San Diego, and I thrive in environments where data, strategy, and execution converge.
